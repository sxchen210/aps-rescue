# Pattern: combined planning blast

**Trigger.** ~6 days before a vet batch day (and often straddling the next
adoption event): one blast that bundles operations.

**Body beats (coordinator, to all affected fosters).**
1. Vet-day heads-up: your dog(s) are scheduled for spay/neuter on {weekday,
   date} with the partner vet — detailed confirmation email with drop-off /
   pickup instructions follows ~4 days before; new fosters told to watch
   for it and reply.
2. Photos + bios demand: "I need PHOTOS and BIOS for your dogs so we can
   get them posted — please send by {weekday} at the latest."
3. Resource pointer: volunteer photographer available for pro shots, with
   her quirk (doesn't drive — you meet her); supplies at the safehouse.

**Replies.** Clarification one-liners ("Are both {Dog} and {Dog} scheduled
for the 16th?"), content-free quoted-tail acks from mobile clients,
deadline-negotiation ("photos this weekend ok?").

**State encoded.** Links dogs → upcoming vet date and dogs → posting
deadline in one artifact; a good stress test for extraction (two workflows
in one email).

**Shape.** 1 blast + 5–12 short replies over 2 days.
