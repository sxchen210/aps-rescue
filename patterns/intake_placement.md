# Pattern: intake and placement

**Intake announcement (coordinator).** A pull from the partner shelter is
announced to candidate fosters: how many dogs coming, ages/sizes/mix in a
sentence each (name, est. age, size, temperament first-read), pickup window
at home base, what's done at intake (basic vaccines, deworming, flea
treatment), what's unknown (history; "no records came with them").

**Placement offers (1:1, coordinator → foster).** Matches a specific dog to
a specific household against known constraints (size pref, kids, other
pets, puppy-tolerance from their application or history): "Would you be
open to taking {Dog}? {one-line pitch}." Foster accepts/declines/negotiates
timing; accept → pickup logistics at home base (day, time window, supplies
available).

**Coverage swaps (vacations).** A fostering household goes away →
coordinator finds a temp or permanent transfer: 1:1 ask naming dates, then
a connecting email between the two fosters for the handoff (see
volunteer_handoff).

**Returns.** Occasionally a placement doesn't work (dog too much for the
household) → apologetic foster email → gracious coordinator reply → dog
back to safehouse → re-placement offer to another household.

**State encoded.** Dog→foster assignment with dates; household
availability; pickup events; constraint matching that later questions test
("who has {Dog} right now?", "who's free this weekend?").

**Shape.** Intake batch: 1 announcement + ~1 offer-thread per dog (2–3
messages each). Swaps/returns: 2–4 messages.
