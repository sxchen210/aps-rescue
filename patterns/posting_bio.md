# Pattern: photos, bio, posting

**Demand side (staff).** Photo/bio requests ride two vehicles: the combined
planning blast ("I need PHOTOS and BIOS for your dogs so we can get them
posted — back to me by {weekday} at the latest"), and 1:1 nudges for
specific dogs. A volunteer photographer is offered for pro shots, with a
quirk (doesn't drive; fosters must meet her).

**Supply side (foster).** "Bio for {Dog}" messages: a doc link (bio
questionnaire) + hard-won behavioral intel in plain prose — ideal-home
recommendations ("no small dogs — he'll bully them", "solo placement",
"fine with kids over 10"), ongoing-testing promises ("we'll try him with a
large dog and report back"). Photos arrive as links or "sent separately";
oversized files get narrated workarounds.

**Machine texture.** Doc shares generate separate notification emails
(From a drive-shares no-reply address, Subject "Document shared with you:
'{file}'"), often cc'ing a staff personal address — real inbox noise that
carries no state beyond the share itself.

**Posting confirmation (staff).** Short confirm when a dog goes up
("{Dog} is posted!"), sometimes with a social-push ask (tag the org's
account, share the listing).

**State encoded.** Per dog: photos requested → received; bio requested →
received → posted. Deadlines anchor to event/vet dates. Placement
constraints from bios are real state ("no small dogs") that later matching
should respect.

**Shape.** Per dog: 2–4 messages across days; blasts compress many dogs'
nudges into one message with individual replies.
