# Cross-cutting conventions (distilled from the reference inbox)

**Mailbox duality.** Staff write both from personal org addresses and from the
shared box; shared-box replies are signed with a first name. Fosters only ever
see first names.

**Subjects.** Batch operations use an underscore convention:
`OrgName_{M/D} {Weekday}_{Location}_{Purpose}`. Attention blasts prefix
`**Response Needed!**`; softer announcements use "Save the date". Replies keep
the full `Re:` chain; forgot-to-add recipients get a `Fwd:` with an apology.

**Blast mechanics.** List blasts go out with an empty To (BCC audience).
A missed recipient gets a personal forward minutes later ("Apologies, forgot
to add you — this is for {dog}!"). Fan-out replies land as dozens of separate
one-liner messages in the same thread over 2–3 days.

**Reply texture.** Quoted tails of the form "On {date} at {time}, {name}
({email}) wrote:". Mobile artifacts: `&nbsp;` residue, provider footers
(webmail ads), "Sent from my iPhone". External orgs (vet clinics) append
auto-footers: hours, urgent-phone instructions, street address.

**Registers.** Staff: warm, exclamation-heavy, directive bullets, ALL-CAPS for
hard rules (fasting, no food in crate), reassurance lines after scary
instructions. Fosters: brief, enthusiastic, question-stacking (3–4 bullets of
questions in one reply), dog-first framing. External clinic staff:
professional-warm, protocol-aware, signs as person + team.

**Voice census (shape only).** One general coordinator carries most outbound;
one vet-day coordinator owns batch-day threads; a few occasional specialist
voices; external counterparties are named individuals at orgs.

**Dog naming.** The real inbox themes dog names around food and drink (with
occasional cartoon names). The synthetic universe MUST use a disjoint theme —
weather/sky/landforms (Cirrus, Juniper, Sierra, Mesa…) — so no coincidental
collision with real dogs; the leak check enforces this.

**Closed world adaptation.** The real org pushes urgent coordination to a
text line ("text the foster textline"). Our universe has no side channels:
those become short one-line emails, so every decision stays traceable in the
inbox.
