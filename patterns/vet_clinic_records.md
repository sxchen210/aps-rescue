# Pattern: coordinator ↔ clinic records handoff

**Participants.** General coordinator and the external clinic's front desk
(a named person signing as "{Name}, {Clinic} Team", with the clinic's
auto-footer: email-for-non-urgent note, office + emergency phone, address).
Not foster-facing.

**Opener (coordinator → clinic), one dense message.**
- Dog's age and provenance in a sentence (returned adoption, shelter pull,
  unknown history).
- What's known/unknown about medical history; what was done at intake
  (vaccine list, deworming, flea treatment); attachment reference
  (certificate/records).
- Current concern stated plainly + any meds already started.
- Pre-authorization: "I authorize {procedure/diagnostic} if Dr. {name} feels
  it's warranted."

**Clinic reply.**
- Warm, personal; often acknowledges a phone/schedule crossover ("saw you
  schedule the appointment as I was writing").
- Protocol notes tied to the concern (e.g., wait-in-car rule when anything
  possibly contagious).
- Notes what will be flagged for the doctor.
- Sometimes a separate follow-up with a small logistics ask (shift the
  appointment 15 minutes, "if not, no worries!").

**Coordinator close.** One-liner acceptance ("Yes, no problem!").

**Shape.** 2–4 messages, clustered same-day; also appears as individual
specialty appointments (dental etc.) with the same anatomy.
