# Pattern: foster application

**Form arrival (machine-generated).** The application form submits as an
email: From a staff address with the org display name, To several org
addresses AND the applicant (cc'd on their own submission). Subject: "Thank
you for your foster application, {Full Name}". Body is the raw Q&A dump:
why-foster, name, street address, city/state/zip, cell, email, how often do
you check email, head of household, occupation, region, can-you-transport-to
home base (where pickups/vet transports/biweekly events happen), ready in
5–7 days, home type, yard, kids' ages, other pets, breed/size preferences,
schedule (hours alone), prior experience.

**Ack (staff, within hours–1 day).** Short: thanks for applying; "while
you're waiting for a volunteer for your interview" + program-material doc
link; invitation to name a good time to chat. Signed either with a first
name or as the team.

**Q&A / screening loop (0–4 turns).** Applicant volunteers household detail
(kids' schedule, apartment, hours away, energy preferences: "no puppies,
medium dogs"). Coordinator answers honestly; outcomes:
- proceed → interview scheduling (specific day/time proposed), then approval
  and first-placement coordination;
- soft decline, two warm sentences: "it sounds like this set-up wouldn't
  work very well for our needs. I do appreciate you reaching out…" — no
  argument, no detail.

**Shape.** 2–6 messages over 1–4 days. ~30/month; maybe a third proceed to
fostering within the window, a third stall (no reply after ack), a third get
screened out or self-select out.
