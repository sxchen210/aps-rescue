# Pattern: batch spay/neuter day

**Trigger.** Recurring fix-day at the partner clinic in a neighboring city,
Wednesdays. Affected fosters = those whose dogs are on the list.

**Blast (T-3 days, vet-day coordinator).**
- Subject: underscore convention with date, weekday, location, purpose.
- Body beats, in order: why you're receiving this (your foster is being fixed
  on {date}); volunteers will transport from home base to the clinic and
  back, so DEFAULT is drop-off and pickup at home base (~6:30AM); two opt-in
  variants, each requiring an ASAP reply — (a) overnight drop the night
  before, after 9PM, dog sleeps at the safehouse; (b) direct drop at the
  clinic Wednesday morning (~7:30AM, pickup 3–4PM); fasting rule in
  caps/emphasis — nothing by mouth from midnight (no food, treats, water);
  a reassurance line ("your animal will be perfectly fine").

**Per-foster confirmation (coordinator, after a foster opts in).**
- Restates the chosen variant and its timing.
- Crate protocol block: crate clearly labeled with the dog's name on top;
  disposable towel or pee pads at the bottom (grab from the safehouse); NO
  food/water/snacks in the crate; last potty break before crating; leave the
  crate at a designated spot in the main area.
- Ends by asking the foster to confirm the timing works.

**Foster replies (1–3 messages).**
- Pickup-time negotiation ("can I come at 8:30 instead?").
- Opportunistic medical add-on: "since {dog} will be at the vet anyway, can
  they look at {minor concern}?" — described observationally (gait, chewing,
  scratching), no diagnosis.
- Post-op question stacks, bulleted: cone — provided or from the safehouse?
  pain meds — where to pick up, how often? activity — calming options for an
  energetic dog?

**Close.** Coordinator answers inline/numbered, short, exclamation-warm.
Direct-drop fosters get the leave-your-phone-number tip (clinic often
finishes early) and the cone + pain meds pickup reminder.

**Fan-out.** One blast → one confirmation thread per opting foster; roughly
half the threads carry at least one question beyond logistics.
