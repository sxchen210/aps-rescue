# Pattern: volunteer logistics handoff ("connecting you")

**Trigger.** A physical item or dog needs a multi-leg relay (supplies to the
clinic, dog between fosters, equipment across towns).

**Opener (coordinator → two volunteers, both in To).**
- Subject: "Connecting you — re: {thing}".
- Addresses each person by name with their constraint/geography in one
  sentence each ("X works near A and lives in B, so she'll bring it over
  after work").
- Proposes the chain concretely (who picks up what, from whom, when, where
  it lands) and asks feasibility.
- Self-aware uncertainty is in-voice ("I have no idea how far apart you two
  are!"). Signs first name.

**Volunteer reply (reply-all).**
- Accepts, shares a phone number for direct coordination, invites the other
  party to make contact when ready. (Closed-world adaptation: the "text me"
  step becomes "shoot me a quick email".)

**Shape.** 2–3 messages, same-day resolution; coordinator drops out once the
volunteers self-organize.
