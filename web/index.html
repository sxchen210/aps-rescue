Animal Protective Services — self-building structured data over a shared inbox

…

Animal Protective Services is a fictional dog rescue, built to mirror the day-to-day operations of a real local rescue. Operations like these actually run across many channels (Gmail, text threads, WhatsApp groups); for this demonstration, everything flows through one shared Gmail inbox. That inbox is where the operational state lives: who has which dog, which placements are confirmed versus tentative, what falls apart when one foster backs out. Normally, someone reconstructs all of that from scrollback before every decision.

This site is a working prototype of the alternative: connect a model to the inbox and let it build the understanding of the operation. A frontier model discovered the schema from ~1,000 emails in the real inbox, blind (no predefined schema, no custom extraction rules); a cheap model has maintained every record since, from each new email, at about $0.0004 per ingestion. Every table view renders from the discovered schema.json; nothing is hand-built per table.

Simulate: how the ~1,000-email world was constructed. A seeded simulator voicing real-inbox patterns behind a strict no-leak boundary, with ground truth kept only for grading.
Discovery: the frontier model exploring the inbox cold and designing the schema, replayed from the committed transcript, round by round with live costs.
Populate & Maintain: a cheap model, following the extraction playbook the frontier model wrote, built these tables from every email and keeps them current; deliver the next email and watch it process, live.
Head-to-head: the same model answering an operational question with and without the tables, live, side by side.
