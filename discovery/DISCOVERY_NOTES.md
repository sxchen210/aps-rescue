## What this operation is
APS Rescue is a small foster-based dog rescue in Redwood City, CA ("the den" = their safehouse). They pull dogs weekly from **San Benito County Animal Services**, place them with volunteer fosters, get them spayed/neutered at **Crestview Veterinary Clinic (Livermore)**, collect photos + a bio questionnaire, post the dog for adoption (rule: photos + bio + fixed = posted), run roughly biweekly Saturday adoption events at **Bark & Barrel Pet Supply**, and schedule adoption appointments at the den. Dogs are named after nature words (Nettle, Rime, Virga, Scarp…), which makes `dog_name` a reliable canonical key. Fosters are private individuals emailing from personal addresses; their display name/signature is stable and matches how staff refer to them, making `foster_name` a workable canonical key.

**Staff (all send from the shared address, distinguished by signature):**
- **Dana** — coordinator: shelter pulls, foster matching, returns, coverage matchmaking, adoption appointments.
- **Priya** — vet-day logistics (spay/neuter transport).
- **June** — adoption postings/roundups.
- **Tom** — foster application intake.
- Also: Opal (volunteer photographer, mentioned), Noel (Crestview front desk).

## Design decisions
- **5 tables.** `dogs` absorbs matching, returns, coverage, vet-day logistics, health notes, and the posting pipeline because every one of those facts is per-dog and the dog name is always present. **`fosters` (new this revision)** is the person-level roster the operator asked for: status lifecycle `active → away → active` (with dated away windows) or `active → departed` (moving out of state, dated), plus recency fields for declines and returns. `foster_applications` stays separate: it's a different lifecycle with different fields, and most applicants never become fosters. `events` + `event_rsvps` separate because RSVP rows are keyed per (event, dog).
- **"Which fosters are free right now?"** = fosters.status `active`, today outside their away window, and no `dogs` row pointing at them via `foster_name`/`coverage_foster`. **Current load is deliberately a JOIN, not a column**: a per-email extractor with no database access cannot maintain a multi-dog list or counter on the person row (it can't read-modify-write), while the `dogs` table already records every placement/coverage with the foster's exact name. So the fosters table stores only person-level facts the emails literally state, and name strings are kept identical across `fosters.foster_name`, `dogs.foster_name`, `dogs.coverage_foster`, and `event_rsvps.foster_name` for the join.
- **"Who is away and until when?"** = `away_start_date`/`away_end_date`, always the STATED window (coverage requests give exact ranges, e.g. Lena 9/24–10/2), never email dates. Vague windows ("until mid-October", Bianca `1a10d4b5902bfed0`) stay as text in `last_decline_reason`/note rather than a fabricated date.
- **"Who recently declined or returned?"** = `last_declined_dog`/`last_decline_date`/`last_decline_reason` and `last_returned_dog`/`last_return_date`. These are the one documented exception to the dated-fields rule: a decline/return announcement IS the event, so the email's own date is the event date (unless a hand-back date is stated). Three decline kinds are explicitly disambiguated in the playbook: dog-offer decline (fosters), application decline (foster_applications), event decline (event_rsvps).
- **Roster population rule** (keeps fosters rows flowing without a registration email): any email whose sender is acting as a foster — accepting, covering, vet-day opt-in, photos/bio, health report, event RSVP, coverage request — refreshes their row (name, email, status), idempotently. Applicants, adopters, staff, clinics, vendors, and the public are excluded by rule.
- **Dated fields over status text** wherever the mailbox states dates: intake_date, spay_neuter_date, adoption_appt_date, foster_pickup_date, coverage window, away window, departure_date, event_date, intro_call_date. Vet days, appointments, events, and away windows are always announced days in advance — email sent dates would be wrong.
- **Hard cases covered in instructions:** multi-dog broadcast (one op per dog), one email carrying two/three workflows (vet opt-in + health concern; RSVP + health + fosters refresh; Dana's coverage matchmaking = two fosters ops + one dogs op), in-email self-correction (`1a107ae748504560` msg 15), adoption reversal/returns with the new `clear` list (`1a10c90704aa3679`), re-offers after declines, adopter-vs-foster distinction, applicant-vs-foster distinction, machine-generated mail (form emails = extractable; Drive pings & promos = noise).
- No "approved" application email was ever observed, and no explicit "adopted — done!" email exists either; adoption completion is inferred from adopter updates. The instructions tell the extractor exactly how to handle both.
